How we work

Four rules every product follows

AI drafts, people decide

In Puple the AI suggests next year's event dates and prices and pre-checks insurance documents. A person accepts each change. Nothing publishes on its own.

Your data stays yours

Customers own their data and can export it. Payments go straight to their own Stripe or Square account, and Puple takes no cut.

Built for a staff of three

Our customers are small teams with no IT department. If they can't run it themselves, we haven't finished.

Useful before impressive

We start with the boring job that eats an afternoon, and we label what is coming instead of promising it.
